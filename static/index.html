Open Voice Engine

Reference client with 16 kHz PCM capture and AudioWorklet playback. Interruption is automatic: speak while the assistant is talking and the server's VAD barges in — there's no interrupt button, tune the VAD below instead.

ⓘ what does this do?
Voice activity detection (barge-in sensitivity)

Higher = harder to trigger, ignores more background noise but misses quiet speech; lower = triggers more easily, but noise/breath sounds may falsely count as speech.

Higher = counts quieter mic input as still-speaking, so trailing breath/hum can delay speech_ended; lower = more sensitive to true silence, but a soft word can look like a pause and end the turn early.

Higher = fewer false triggers from clicks/coughs, but adds delay before barge-in reacts; lower = near-instant barge-in, but more prone to firing on brief noise blips.

Higher = tolerates longer pauses mid-sentence without cutting you off, but adds latency before the assistant responds; lower = snappier turn-taking, but normal pauses may get treated as the end of your turn.
